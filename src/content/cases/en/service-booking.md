## The idea

The goal was to choose and book a maintenance service the way people buy online, without a phone call. The prototype put that idea in front of people, so the discussion could start from something that works.

## What I built

A complete, responsive flow for desktop and mobile:

- search and a service catalog, with what is included, duration and an illustrative price;
- booking at the workshop or on the farm, with branch, date and period;
- machine and customer details, ending in a protocol number;
- "My requests", so the customer can follow along;
- "Workshop view", to show the operational value for the people serving customers.

## Why it stopped at a prototype

Data lives only in memory, with no database and no integrations. The aim was to validate the flow before investing in those parts.
