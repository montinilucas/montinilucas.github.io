## The problem

The management dashboards ran on QlikView, with years of rules buried in load scripts. Changing tools without understanding those rules would mean trading trusted numbers for similar-looking ones.

## How I built it

- **Qlik is evidence, not the source.** I read the Qlik scripts and files to understand each rule, but the official source remains the ERP.
- **Nothing is assumed.** An ambiguous rule is logged as a business question until the team that owns it confirms.
- **One full path before scaling.** I started with a vertical MVP and then moved domain by domain: Parts, Machines, Service, Finance, Accounting and Consolidated.
- **Reports generated by code.** Semantic models and reports are built by scripts, so a release from development to production can be repeated, with an automatic check in DAX.

## The checking rule

Every metric must close the chain `QlikView = Gold = Power BI measure`, with a 0.1% tolerance, by total, period, branch and area.

When it does not close, the difference is investigated until it has an explanation. Sometimes it is a different cutoff date, sometimes stale data in the old dashboard. Some domains still have differences under investigation, which is why this project's status is "under review".
